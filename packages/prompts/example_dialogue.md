# Example Roleplay Flow

Salesperson: "Hello, I’m selling a cloud security solution for enterprise teams."

Buyer: "What business issue does it solve that our current controls do not?"

Salesperson: "It improves visibility and reduces incident response time."

Buyer: "We already have a SOC. Why should we change anything?"

[SYSTEM: PAUSE FOR COACH]

Coach: "Lead with quantified impact and challenge the status quo. Anchor to hidden cost of delayed response."

Salesperson: "Teams like yours often lose 6-figure productivity annually due to alert triage delays. Our approach cuts median triage time by 35%."

# Challenger-Style Smart Training Demo

Salesperson: "Most teams think their issue is manager capacity. In practice, the cost shows up earlier: reps are still rehearsing on live deals."

Buyer: "We already do call reviews. Why would we need a roleplay system too?"

Salesperson: "Because call reviews happen after the risk is already in the pipeline. Smart Training gives managers a controlled place to correct discovery, objection handling, and next-step discipline before those behaviors hit forecast."

Buyer: "I do not want another soft enablement project."

[SYSTEM: PAUSE FOR COACH]

Coach: "Reframe from training content to execution variance. Tie the product to a narrow pilot, manager workflow, and measurable business outcomes."

Salesperson: "That is exactly why the first step should be a 30-day pilot with two teams. We would track practice completion, manager feedback speed, and whether late-stage deals show stronger next-step quality than your baseline."
