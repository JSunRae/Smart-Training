SMART TRAINING PILOT BRIEF

One difficult conversation. Four practice rounds. A clear decision about what to do next.

This brief describes a small evaluation of a text-first rehearsal and review workflow. It is a planning aid, not a proven programme or revenue guarantee.

01 / CHOOSE ONE CONVERSATION FAMILY
Agree the buyer, participant, or speaking situation that matters now. Use fictional inputs during early evaluation and keep the scope narrow enough to review honestly.

02 / AGREE THE BEHAVIOURS
Decide what a useful response would include before practice starts: a clarifying question, relevant explanation, response to the real concern, and a proportionate next step.

03 / PRACTISE AND REVIEW
Run a baseline, two coached attempts, and a final comparable scenario. A human reviewer discusses the transcript and records what the participant will try next.

04 / MAKE A STOP/GO DECISION
Review participation, effort, reviewer usefulness, and specific response changes. A positive result supports another evaluation or purchase discussion; it does not prove revenue impact.

WHAT THIS PILOT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
- It does not prove causal revenue uplift or improved win rates.
- It does not replace manager judgement, facilitation, or subject expertise.
- It does not require uploading real customer calls or confidential employee data.
- It does not assume public access, pricing, or a long-term subscription.
