# Vertical Pack: Cybersecurity and Infrastructure

Last updated: 2026-05-15

## Recommendation

Use this as the **default external demo pack** after the core Smart Training seller pack.

Why:

- It fits the current product story: complex B2B deals, multistakeholder buying, skeptical technical buyers, and measurable business tension.
- It aligns with markets already under pressure to improve AI adoption, seller quality, and buyer confidence.
- It gives you executive, operational, and technical buyer examples without requiring heavy regulated messaging.

## Who To Target

Primary personas:

1. VP of Sales at cybersecurity or infrastructure vendors
2. CRO at cloud, data, observability, or security-platform companies
3. Sales enablement leaders responsible for first-line manager adoption
4. RevOps leaders responsible for forecast quality on complex deals

## What Problem To Lead With

Do not lead with:

- AI roleplay
- coaching at scale
- practice content

Lead with:

- reps are still rehearsing on live enterprise opportunities
- managers coach too late, after risk has already entered pipeline
- inspection exposes weak discovery, but does not fix it upstream

## Seeded Showcase Scenarios

Use these first:

1. `Zero Trust Access Modernization`
2. `Cloud Resilience Budget Pressure`
3. `Forecast Confidence Through Coaching`
4. `Frontline Coaching Consistency`

## Best Demo Path

### 8-minute path

1. Open `Frontline Coaching Consistency`.
2. Show one weak seller answer and trigger coach pause.
3. Reframe the product as a manager-consistency system, not a content library.
4. Open `Zero Trust Access Modernization` to prove the platform handles hard technical buying tension.

### 15-minute path

1. Start with `Forecast Confidence Through Coaching` for RevOps.
2. Move to `Zero Trust Access Modernization` for executive skepticism.
3. Use `Cloud Resilience Budget Pressure` to show budget and tooling rationalization objections.
4. Reopen a saved transcript and explain the pilot scorecard.

## Example Challenger Hooks

### Security platform leader

- "Your reps do not lose security deals because they lack product slides. They lose them because hard risk objections are still being handled live for the first time."
- "The hidden cost is not just a missed deal. It is inconsistent narrative quality across the team on the most expensive opportunities."

### Infrastructure leader

- "Your dashboards tell you where incidents hurt. They do not tell you whether your sellers can explain the cost of delay well enough to win internal buy-in."
- "If resilience is a board conversation, seller quality becomes a business risk, not an enablement side topic."

## Pilot Metrics

Use a narrow pilot with:

1. two frontline teams
2. one objection family
3. one manager workflow
4. a 30-day window

Track:

1. practice completion
2. manager feedback turnaround
3. objection-handling score quality
4. next-step quality in active deals
5. late-stage slippage trends on pilot reps

## Testing Prompts

Use these in live demos:

1. Weak response test:
   "We help teams improve coaching and visibility across the funnel."
   Expected outcome: buyer stays unconvinced, coach should push toward quantified business tension.
2. Strong response test:
   "Most teams inspect deal risk after it shows up in pipeline. We help managers correct the behavior that creates that risk before the next live call."
   Expected outcome: buyer should shift from dismissal to diagnostic interest.
