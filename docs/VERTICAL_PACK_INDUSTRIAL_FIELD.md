# Vertical Pack: Industrial and Field Services

Last updated: 2026-05-15

## Recommendation

Use this as the **second expansion pack** after cyber and infrastructure.

Why:

- It gives Smart Training a strong operations story, not only a SaaS story.
- Challenger messaging lands well because operational leaders respond to hidden cost, execution variance, and frontline behavior.
- It avoids the heavier compliance posture you would need to market aggressively into medtech first.

## Who To Target

Primary personas:

1. VP of Service Operations
2. Plant Operations Director
3. Commercial leaders at industrial software, automation, field-service, and equipment vendors
4. Enablement leaders supporting distributed field or regional teams

## What Problem To Lead With

Do not position the product as generic training.

Position it as:

- a way to reduce execution variance before expensive field interactions
- a manager reinforcement system for distributed teams
- a practice workflow tied to uptime, escalation quality, first-time-fix discipline, and rollout adoption

## Seeded Showcase Scenarios

Use these first:

1. `Field Service Downtime Prevention`
2. `Plant Automation Change Resistance`
3. `Ramp Time Compression`
4. `Change Management Adoption`

## Best Demo Path

### 8-minute path

1. Open `Field Service Downtime Prevention`.
2. Give a generic seller answer and trigger coach pause.
3. Show how the product redirects the rep toward uptime, repeat truck rolls, and escalation cost.
4. Explain that the same workflow can be used by distributed managers without waiting for live customer failures.

### 15-minute path

1. Start with `Plant Automation Change Resistance`.
2. Move to `Field Service Downtime Prevention`.
3. Reopen a completed transcript to show persistence and review.
4. Close on a regional pilot with one frontline manager scorecard.

## Example Challenger Hooks

### Service operations

- "The issue is not only dispatch discipline. It is what happens when technicians hit the same customer objections live and handle them differently every time."
- "Every repeat truck roll is partly an execution problem, not just a scheduling problem."

### Plant operations

- "Most digital rollouts fail because supervisors cannot reinforce the behavior under production pressure."
- "If the workflow is skipped when output is on the line, the problem is not adoption messaging. It is unpracticed frontline behavior."

## Pilot Metrics

Track:

1. practice completion on the pilot team
2. manager feedback turnaround
3. objection-handling consistency
4. escalation quality
5. first-time-fix proxy metrics or repeat-visit trends
6. supervisor reinforcement cadence

## Testing Prompts

Use these in demos:

1. Weak response test:
   "This gives your field team better training content and more practice."
   Expected outcome: buyer sees it as generic.
2. Strong response test:
   "Your technicians are still learning the hardest stakeholder conversations in front of customers. We help managers correct that before the next site visit."
   Expected outcome: buyer should engage on operational risk and pilot structure.
