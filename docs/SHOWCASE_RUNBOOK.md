# Showcase Runbook

Last updated: 2026-05-15

Use this runbook when you want a repeatable demo without improvising the whole conversation.

## Scenario 1: Frontline Coaching Consistency

Best audience:

1. VP of Sales
2. Sales enablement
3. Frontline manager leader

Weak first response:

`We help managers coach more often with AI roleplay and transcript review.`

Strong Challenger response:

`Most managers are coaching after calls go wrong. We give them a controlled place to correct objection handling before that risk shows up in pipeline.`

What this should prove:

1. The product is not just content delivery.
2. The coach pause creates a visible before/after moment.

## Scenario 2: Forecast Confidence Through Coaching

Best audience:

1. RevOps
2. CRO
3. VP Sales

Weak first response:

`This helps reps practice and should improve pipeline quality over time.`

Strong Challenger response:

`Inspection tells you where risk is. Practice tells you how to stop creating it in the first place. We use controlled roleplay to improve the discovery and objection behavior that later shows up as slippage.`

What this should prove:

1. Smart Training complements CRM and inspection systems.
2. The pitch can move from enablement language to operating language.

## Scenario 3: Zero Trust Access Modernization

Best audience:

1. Security platform seller teams
2. Cyber GTM leaders
3. Enterprise AE training buyers

Weak first response:

`We modernize identity and help you implement zero trust faster.`

Strong Challenger response:

`The hidden cost is not just architecture complexity. It is the constant exception handling, third-party access drift, and audit friction that keeps security leaders trapped in cleanup mode.`

What this should prove:

1. The simulator can handle skeptical technical buyers.
2. The rep is pushed to teach, not just pitch.

## Scenario 4: Field Service Downtime Prevention

Best audience:

1. Service operations
2. Industrial software leaders
3. Distributed-team managers

Weak first response:

`We improve training consistency for field teams.`

Strong Challenger response:

`Every repeat truck roll is a margin leak. Dispatch matters, but so does what your technician says when the customer pushes back on scope, parts, or timing.`

What this should prove:

1. The product applies outside classic SaaS.
2. The business case can be tied to operational outcomes.
